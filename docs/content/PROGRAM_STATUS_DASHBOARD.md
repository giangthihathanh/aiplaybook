## Dashboard footnote

Green indicates that the required program foundation or controlled template is available. Amber indicates that an activity is incomplete or requires evidence. Green does not mean that a POC is approved for scale. Scale requires an overall score of at least 80, Governance Gate PASS, minimum sample completion, reproducible material KPI values, accountable ownership, and no unresolved critical incident.
